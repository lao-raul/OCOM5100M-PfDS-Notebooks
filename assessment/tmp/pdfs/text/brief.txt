     Purpose                                                                        
                                                                                    
     The purpose of this assessment is to develop your skills in devising and presenting a Data Science project.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     Assessment    Task                                                             
                                                                                    
     Part 1 – Data Analysis Project                                                 
     For Part 1, you must choose your own dataset, and it must be publicly available and suitable for exploratory
     data analysis. Recommended sources include:                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         • Kaggle (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets)                                 
         • UCI Machine Learning Repository (https://archive.ics.uci.edu/)           
         • scikit-learn sample datasets (https://scikit-learn.org/stable/datasets.html)
         • Open data portals and APIs (e.g. government, WHO, World Bank)            
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     You may use scikit-learn only to load a built-in dataset (e.g. sklearn.datasets). You must not use the library for
     any other part of your analysis in this section: no preprocessing, transformations, or statistical functions from
     scikit-learn. Part 1 should otherwise rely on core Python, pandas, and a visualisation library only; the full scikit-
                                                                                    
     learn toolkit is reserved for Part 2.                                          
                                                                                    
     Part 2 – Machine Learning Pipeline                                             
     For Part 2 you will work with a fixed dataset provided by the module team. This dataset supports a binary
     classification problem, contains a mixture of numerical and categorical features, and includes a clearly defined
                                                                                    
     target variable. The exact dataset and filename for the current run will be provided in the notebook template.
                                                                                    
     Part 1 – Data Analysis Project (40 marks)

     Section 1.1 – Project Aim and Dataset Description (10 marks)                   
     State your project aim and define two specific, measurable objectives that are relevant to your chosen dataset
     and domain. Write a description of your dataset covering its source, contents, quality, and suitability for your
                                                                                    
     intended analysis.                                                             
                                                                                    
     Expected length: approximately 400–500 words                                   
                                                                                    
     Section 1.2 – Exploratory Data Analysis and Code (12 marks)                    
     Write well-structured Python code to explore your dataset. Your code should be logically organised, clearly
                                                                                    
     commented, and easy to follow. Use descriptive variable names and include comments that explain why each
     step is being performed. After each block of code, include a brief markdown cell explaining what the output
     shows and what it means for your analysis.                                     
                                                                                    
     The combined code for Sections 1.2 and 1.3 must not exceed 250 non-empty lines. The written
                                                                                    
     commentary for this section 1.2 must not exceed 600 words. These are maximum limits rather than
     recommended targets; quality, relevance and clarity matter more than quantity. 
                                                                                    
     Section 1.3 – Objectives Analysis (6 marks)                                    
     For each objective, present your analysis and findings supported by at least one visualisation. You must:
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
         • Quote at least one specific numerical value visible in the visualisation or output (for example, a
          peak value, a proportion, a correlation coefficient, or an outlier value).
         • Explain what that value means in the context of the domain, not just in terms of the data.
                                                                                    
     Expected length: approximately 250–325 words per objective, plus visualisations.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     Section 1.4 – Insights and Reflections (4 marks)                               
     Write a summary of your analysis covering:                                     
                                                                                    
         • Your main findings.                                                      
         • Anything unexpected you encountered.                                     
                                                                                    
         • The limitations of your work.                                            
                                                                                    
     Expected length: approximately 200–250 words.

      Total                                 32 Marks                                
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     Section 2.1 – Preprocessing and Pipelines (10 marks)                           
                                                                                    
     Prepare the dataset and build a scikit-learn preprocessing pipeline. After the code, write a reflection of
     approximately 200–250 words justifying the preprocessing decisions you made for this dataset.
                                                                                    
     Section 2.2 – Baseline Model Training and Evaluation (4 marks)                 
                                                                                    
     Train two classification models as baselines. For each model, build a complete pipeline that incorporates your
     preprocessor. After the code, justify your choice of classifiers. Expected length: approximately 100–150 words.
                                                                                    
     Section 2.3 – Cross-Validation (6 marks)                                       
     Evaluate both baseline pipelines using cross-validation. Report at least one metric beyond accuracy, including
                                                                                    
     mean and standard deviation across folds.                                      
                                                                                    
     After the code, write a reflection of approximately 200–250 words comparing the two models and discussing
     what the results indicate about performance and stability. Identify which model you would take forward and
     justify your choice.                                                           
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
     Section 2.4 – Hyperparameter Tuning (6 marks)                                  
     Select one model to tune. Apply a hyperparameter search to the full pipeline and report the best configuration
     found. Compare the tuned performance with your baseline results from Section 2.3.
                                                                                    
     After the code, write a reflection of approximately 200–250 words justifying your choices and evaluating
                                                                                    
     whether tuning produced a meaningful improvement.                              
                                                                                    
     Section 2.5 – Final Model Evaluation and Justification (6 marks)               
     Evaluate your final model on the held-out test set. Report appropriate metrics, including precision, recall and
                                                                                    
     F1, and display a confusion matrix with labelled axes. Compare the test performance with your earlier cross-
     validation results.                                                            
                                                                                    
                                                                                    

===== PAGE 5 =====

                                                                                    
     After the code, write a justification of approximately 250-300 words interpreting the results, discussing how
                                                                                    
     well the model generalises, and providing a reasoned recommendation on whether the model is fit for
     purpose or where further improvement is needed.                                
                                                                                    
     Process Log (12 marks)                                                         
                                                                                    
     Submit a process log of no more than 400 words as part of your notebook, in the cell provided at the end of
     the template.                                                                  
                                                                                    
     Your process log must address all three of the following:                      
                                                                                    
         • Dead ends: Describe at least two things you tried from parts 1 and 2 that did not work during
                                                                                    
          your analysis or modelling. Be specific.                                  
         • Surprising finding: Identify one finding from your analysis that you did not expect. Reference
          the specific value or output.                                             
         • What you would do differently: If you were starting again with more time, what is the one
                                                                                    
          thing you would change? Ground your answer in your own results.           
                                                                                    
     Report Quality (4 marks)                                                       
     These marks assess the overall presentation and professionalism of your submitted notebook, independent of
                                                                                    
     the technical content assessed elsewhere. Specifically:                        
                                                                                    
         • All template placeholder and instructional text has been removed, leaving only your own work.
         • The notebook is well-organised, with outputs visible and section headings intact.
         • Writing throughout is clear, grammatically correct, and free of significant typos.
                                                                                    
         • The notebook reads as a coherent, professional piece of work rather than a set of disconnected
          answers.                                                                  
                                                                                    
     General Marking Guidance                                                       
     Marks are awarded for the quality of your reasoning and the clarity of your code and writing, not for achieving
                                                                                    
     high model accuracy. A well-justified decision based on modest results will score more highly than a high-
     performing model with no explanation.                                          
                                                                                    
     Across both parts, marks are awarded for:                                      
                                                                                    
         • Correctness and appropriateness of your code.                            
                                                                                    
         • Quality of written interpretation grounded in specific values from your outputs.
         • Clarity of code structure, comments, and variable naming.                
         • Evidence of critical thinking in your decisions and reflections.         
                                                                                    
         • Consistency between your code, outputs, written commentary.

          what the difference between two preprocessing strategies is or how a particular function
          behaves;

          it means;

         • make analytical decisions on your behalf, for example asking AI which preprocessing strategy,
