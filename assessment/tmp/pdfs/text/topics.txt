       1. Air Accidents    Analysis   (2008-2022)                                   
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
       Explore temporal trends in accident frequency and fatalities across different time periods.
       Analyse patterns by flight phase, aircraft characteristics, and engine types. Investigate
                                                                                    
       geographical distribution of accidents and compare against global air traffic densities. Examine
       correlations between aircraft attributes and safety outcomes through statistical analysis.
                                                                                    
       Dataset: Air Accidents from Jan2008 to May2022                               
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
       2. UK  Online   Retail E-commerce      Data                                  
                                                                                    
       Identify high-performing products and key sales markets to understand demand patterns.
                                                                                    
       Analyse revenue trends across different timeframes to detect seasonal variations and peak
                                                                                    
       periods. Segment customers based on purchasing behaviours including frequency, value, and
       product preferences. Develop insights for inventory optimization and marketing strategies.
                                                                                    
       Dataset: E-commerce data                                                     
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
       3. S&P   500  Stock  Returns   (2009-2019)

       modelling techniques to forecast stock performance using historical data. Identify potential
       leading indicators of company performance.
